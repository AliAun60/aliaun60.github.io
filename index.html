A bit about me

I'm a software engineer focused on building AI-powered applications from the ground up. My senior capstone was a production RAG tutoring platform: a full agentic flow from query to retrieval to LLM generation, with guardrails and evaluation keeping outputs grounded, containerized with Docker and shipped through CI/CD.

Alongside AI work, I have a deep front-end and accessibility background. Over two years in Miami University's IT Services I audited hundreds of digital platforms against WCAG 2.1, doing code-level root-cause analysis across macOS, Windows, iOS, and Android, and trained new hires on the testing methodology.

I like breaking problems to their core and rebuilding them a better way — with strong CS fundamentals in data structures, algorithms, systems, and databases underneath.
